Mallow enclosure — rev D

119 × 78 × 38 mm, 2.5 mm walls, 6.5 mm corner radius, square bottom edge.

Rear openings come from Figure 4 of the datasheet — connector centres 14.20 / 31.60 / 48.99 / 65.79 / 85.29 mm from the board's left edge, widths 15.74 / 14.30 / 8.92 / 15.00 / 17.30, each with 0.6 mm clearance.

The rear panel is 1.5 mm thick and unbroken, so the PCB edge and the bottom-side parts stay hidden. Its outer face sits level with the USB-C: RJ45 and power stand 0.7 mm proud, HDMI sits 0.8 mm in, USB-A 1.8 mm in.

Four solid corner posts live in the 7 mm side gaps, clear of the board. Each takes an M3 from the base plate and carries a 5 × 2 mm magnet flush with the wall top, meeting its pair in the cover.

Board drops straight down onto four standoffs on the base plate at its own Ø3.2 holes — M3 from above, rear pair Ø6 to clear the panel.

Base plate: counterbored screw holes and four Ø10.4 pockets for 10 × 2 mm mounting magnets. M3 pilot bores are Ø2.8.

Front face, from Figure 4: two Ø4 switch windows and two Ø2 LED windows at 9 / 14 / 18 / 23 mm from the board's right edge. Standoffs stay at 10 mm — Figure 7 gives 8.52 mm of bottom-side height.
