Bridgingfront windows, rear openings

The four front windows and the five rear openings bridge across their tops. If your first layer over them sags, print the shell rear-panel-down instead and add supports under the corner posts.

Cover, shell, board, base plate. The cover floats on four magnets; the base plate takes four M3 up into the corner posts. Dimensioned views of each part are on the separate drawing sheet.

Assembly order

  1. Glue four 5 × 2 mm magnets into the corner posts of the shell, faces flush with the wall top. Glue the other four into the cover posts — test each pair with a spare disc before the glue sets, so the polarity pulls rather than pushes.
  2. Screw the shell to the base plate: four M3 × 8 self-tappers up through the counterbored holes into the posts.
  3. Drop the board in from the top. It sits on the four standoffs; the rear connectors line up with the panel openings.
  4. Four M3 × 6 down through the board's Ø3.2 holes into the standoffs.
  5. Optional: glue four 10 × 2 mm discs into the pockets in the base underside if you want the box to stick to steel.
  6. Set the cover on. It floats 3.5 mm above the wall and is held by the magnets alone.
